# Chapter 5 — Maxi (MaximalSelf)

## 5.1 What Is Maxi?

**Maxi, short for MaximalSelf, is the central self that performs a Symphony and coordinates the other selves in the World.**

Maxi does not perform every task itself.

Instead, it understands the structure of a Symphony and determines which selves need to participate.

```text
Intent
  ↓
AI
  ↓
Symphony
  ↓
Maxi
  ↓
Finite Selves
```

## 5.2 Maxi's Instruction Set

Maxi has a small instruction set for understanding and coordinating a Symphony.

It can work with concepts such as:

The actual capabilities are provided by the selves and their computational engines.

Maxi coordinates them.

## 5.3 Maxi and the World

Maxi has access to the World and its selves.

```text
Maxi
├── World
├── Finite Selves
├── CEP
├── Clingo / Planner
├── AI
└── UTM
```

When a Symphony requires several selves to act together, Maxi coordinates their participation.

For example:

```text
Button Self
Chart Self
Workspace Self
AI Self
```

Maxi determines how the Symphony connects them and distributes the required work.

## 5.4 Maxi Does Not Replace the Selves

Maxi is not the Button, Chart, or Spreadsheet.

Those remain independent finite selves.

Maxi coordinates them:

```text
             Maxi
               │
       ┌───────┼───────┐
       ↓       ↓       ↓
    Button   Chart   Spreadsheet
     Self     Self      Self
```

Each self remains responsible for its own state, capabilities, events, and instructions.

## 5.5 The Key Idea

> **Maxi is the central computational self that performs Symphony by coordinating the finite selves that make up the World.**
