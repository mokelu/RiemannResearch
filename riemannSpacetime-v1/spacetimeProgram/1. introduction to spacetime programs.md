On a normal computer or phone, you have apps like: Google Chrome, Facebook, WhatsApp, Calculator, Instagram. You can call them apps or programs. The common word used today is apps.

An app has an icon you tap on your phone or computer, and it then opens. Apps do a specific thing, like send a message, chat, play music, browse, take a picture, etc.

Spacetime programs or spacetime apps do NOT have icons but they are no different. They exist to do a specific thing, like send a message, create a file, etc.

Here is an example of a spacetime program:
```sy
SPACE-TIME PROGRAM

When a patient is admitted,
create a patient record.
then When a doctor approves the patient,
change the patient's status to approved.
When the patient's status becomes approved,
tell the nurse.
When the nurse confirms treatment,
change the patient's status to being_treated.
```

```sy
Workflow:

When a file is uploaded,
scan it.

When the scan finishes,
store the result.

When the result is unsafe,
notify the user.
```
