# What Riemann Research Is

## The Definition

Riemann Research is a serious interactive research environment for asking questions about a defined body of data, evidence, models, and assumptions — and exploring the consequences visually, computationally, and interactively.

That is the whole definition. Nothing more is needed. Nothing more should be added.

## What It Is Not

Riemann Research is not:

- A general-purpose operating system
- A general-purpose coding environment
- A cloud platform
- An autonomous business operator
- A payment system
- A broker
- A bank
- A communications network
- A government platform
- A critical infrastructure operator
- A general-purpose computational substrate
- A chatbot
- A dashboard
- A search engine

The moment it becomes any of these, it has failed its definition.

## The Boundary

The word "research" is the boundary.

Research means: a defined body of data, a question, evidence, assumptions, models, and answers that follow from them. When you stop doing that, you have left Riemann Research.

You can research fantasy football. You can research trading strategies. You can research game economies. You can research markets. You can research sports. You can research anything — as long as it has data, evidence, and questions.

But you cannot research without data. You cannot research without questions. You cannot research without evidence and assumptions. The moment you remove those, you are no longer doing research. You are doing something else. That something else is not Riemann Research.

## How It Grows

Riemann Research grows by adding domains, not by expanding scope.

Version 1: Fantasy research.
Version 2: Trading research.
Version 3: Sports, finance, markets.
Version 4: General research.
Version 5: Millions of specialized research environments.

```
Fantasy football.
Trading research.
Sports analysis.
Scientific research.
Business intelligence.
Market research.
Economic analysis.
Operations research.
Investigative research.
Financial modeling.
Scenario analysis.
Risk analysis.
And potentially many other domains.
```

Each version is a new domain, not a new capability. The underlying machinery stays the same. The definition stays the same. Only the data and the questions change.

## What Stays Hidden

The neurosymbolic runtime that powers Riemann Research is not part of its public identity. It is the engine underneath. Users see the environment, the questions, the evidence, the answers, and the views. They do not see the runtime. They do not need to.

The runtime can grow. The definition cannot.

## The Test

Any feature, any product decision, any new direction must pass this test:

**Is this research?**

If the answer is yes — it involves a question, evidence, assumptions, models, and an answer derived from them — it belongs in Riemann Research.

If the answer is no — it does not involve those things — it does not belong. Build it somewhere else. Let it live somewhere else. But not here.

## The Name

Riemann Research. Named after the Riemann sum — discrete bars composing a continuous curve. Structure from parts. That is what the environment does: it takes discrete pieces of evidence and assumption and composes them into a continuous answer.

The mark is five vertical bars of varying height, rising like a curve. That is the logo. That is the identity. That is enough.

## The Runtime

The runtime is what the files call **COMPANY1** — the neurosymbolic runtime and general-purpose intent programming system.

It is the engine that:

- Takes a natural language question
- Parses it into structured research objects (evidence, assumptions, conditions, models)
- Composes them into a live, interactive answer
- Updates the answer when any input changes

It is the thing that makes the answer recomputable instead of static. When you drag a lever and the answer moves, that is the runtime working. When you toggle evidence and the whole composition updates, that is the runtime working.

**Riemann Research** is what users see: the environment, the question bar, the evidence cards, the adjustable levers, the views.

**The runtime** is what makes it work: the neurosymbolic engine underneath that turns sentences into structured objects and composes them into live answers.

The strategy says: show Riemann Research. Never show the runtime. Let people think it is a clever frontend over standard tools. Let them think the magic is in the UI. Do not explain that the underlying system can do far more than research questions.

The runtime is the real power. Riemann Research is the mask.
