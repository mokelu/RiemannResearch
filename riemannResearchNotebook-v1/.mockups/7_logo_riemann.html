Usage

Do

Use the mark in Riemann Green on Paper or Ink. Keep clear space. Use the horizontal lockup whenever width allows. Scale down to 14 px without loss of legibility. Pair the wordmark with the mark, not with a tagline.

Don't

Don't rotate, skew, or outline the mark. Don't recolor it outside the palette. Don't add gradients, shadows, or glows. Don't place it on busy photographic backgrounds. Don't stretch or condense the wordmark. Don't set the wordmark in a different typeface.
