The Test

    
Any feature must answer one question: is this research?

    
Research means a defined body of data, a question, evidence, assumptions, models — and an answer derived from them. Every card on this page passes: it has a dataset, levers you can move, and an answer that recomputes.

    
Riemann Research is not:

    
    
      
  • an operating system
  • a chatbot
  • a cloud platform
  • a payment system
    • 
      
  • a broker or bank
  • a dashboard
  • a search engine
  • an automation tool
    • 
    

    
The moment it becomes any of these, it has failed its definition. Try the boundary in the ask bar — it will refuse.
