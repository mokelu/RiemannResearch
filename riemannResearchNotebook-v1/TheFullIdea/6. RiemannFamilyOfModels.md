# Riemann 1

Riemann 1 is our own AI model family.

Riemann 1 is not another wrapper around an existing general-purpose LLM. It is a model we design, train, and control ourselves for the kind of reasoning we want our systems to perform.

The first direction for Riemann 1 is System 1: fast, structured judgment rather than long-form generation. Through JEV, a system can present Riemann 1 with a typed question and relevant state, and Riemann 1 produces a structured result such as a choice, score, or noul.

The important idea is that Riemann 1 does not need to compete with ChatGPT by becoming a bigger chatbot. It can be a different kind of model, optimized for fast machine reasoning and decision-making.

Riemann 1 is the model family. Different Riemann 1 models can eventually exist for different capabilities, sizes, or environments.

For example:

```text
Riemann 1
├── Riemann 1 Presto
├── Riemann 1 Hyperstructure
├── Riemann 1 Cherub
└── ...
```

The names and model structure can evolve later. The important decision is establishing Riemann 1 as our own model family.
